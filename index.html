Methodology and limitations

Case-insensitive literal matching with Unicode word boundaries; whitespace normalized; optional accent folding. Longer overlapping name matches take precedence; identical spans retain all candidate identities and classifications. Overlapping textual indicators count once within each category and once in the total across categories. Separate non-overlapping mentions count separately. Index = occurrences / word count; words are Unicode letter/number sequences with internal apostrophes or hyphens. Empty selected fields receive NO PROVENANCE TEXT and blank counts/indexes. Punctuation-only text has undefined indexes. Fields are analysed separately.

Evidence retains category-specific overlapping matches; Counted_in_total marks the non-overlapping subset used for the total index. Consolidated names group by candidate identifier set; ambiguous sets remain separate. Victoria is withheld for review, not deleted from the source dictionary. Comma-delimited aliases are ambiguous. Version 0.2 excludes single-word comma fragments and known ambiguous multiword fragments; other multiword candidates remain provisional. Review the downloadable audit. Museum exclusion uses name/description keywords and is not a verified classification. Formatting repair joins known German words split after umlauts and permits name matches after digits; original text is preserved. No identity is verified by a string match. Prefix indicators “before 19” and “by 19” deliberately match year prefixes. Original input columns are preserved as parsed CSV values. UTF-8 CSV (comma, semicolon or tab) supported. No model or network requests.

Based on the OpenArtData GLAMhack2020 and 2021GLAMHACK projects. Prototype; not a validated research release.
